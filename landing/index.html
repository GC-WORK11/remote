HOW IT WORKS

Sixty seconds
to connected.

One installer on the laptop, one app on the phone, one QR code between them.

01 — INSTALL

Run the installer

One command installs everything — the program, the agent, and the settings that keep it awake with the lid shut.

02 — PAIR

Scan the QR

The installer prints a QR code. The app scans it — that’s the whole login. No passwords, no accounts.

03 — CLOSE THE LID

Work from anywhere

Your laptop stays awake with the lid shut. Same Wi-Fi on the couch, Tailscale from anywhere else.
